# HESI–5P reference register

Last checked: 14 August 2026.

This register identifies the main primary and official sources that inform the HESI–5P Innovation Model and its worked applications. Inclusion does not mean that the source organisation endorses HESI–5P.

Laws, standards, statistics and guidance change. Before making a consequential decision, check the current source, applicable jurisdiction and commencement date, and obtain specialist advice where needed.

## Sustainable development and measurement

- United Nations, [Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development](https://sdgs.un.org/2030agenda). Primary source for the integrated framework of People, Planet, Prosperity, Peace and Partnership.
- United Nations, [The 17 Sustainable Development Goals](https://sdgs.un.org/goals). Official overview of the goals and targets.
- United Nations Statistics Division, [Global indicator framework for the Sustainable Development Goals](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/indicators/indicators-list/). Official indicator list, including the 2025 comprehensive review and 2026 annual refinements.
- United Nations Statistics Division, [SDG indicator metadata repository](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/). Definitions, methods and custodian information for the global indicators.

## Human rights, equality and accessibility

- United Nations, [Universal Declaration of Human Rights](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights). Foundational statement of universal human rights.
- Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights, [Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities](https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/convention-rights-persons-disabilities). International framework for disability rights, accessibility, participation and inclusion.
- World Wide Web Consortium, [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). International technical recommendations for accessible web content.
- UK Parliament, [Equality Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/contents). Principal equality legislation for Great Britain; applicability depends on context and jurisdiction.

## Artificial intelligence and data governance

- European Union, [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689—the Artificial Intelligence Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng). Official Journal text.
- European Union, [Consolidated Artificial Intelligence Act as at 27 July 2026](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng). Consolidated legal text current on the stated date.
- European Commission, [AI Act overview and application timeline](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai). Official implementation overview; verify it alongside the legal text.
- European Commission, [AI Omnibus enters into force](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/ai-omnibus-enters-force). Official notice of the amendments that entered into force on 27 July 2026.
- UNESCO, [Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence). International framework covering human dignity, fairness, transparency, oversight and environmental effects.
- Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development, [OECD AI Principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles). Principles for innovative and trustworthy AI, updated in 2024.
- US National Institute of Standards and Technology, [AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework). Voluntary risk-management framework; NIST states that version 1.0 is being revised.
- UK Information Commissioner’s Office, [Guidance on AI and data protection](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/). UK guidance on applying data-protection principles to AI.
- UK Information Commissioner’s Office and the Alan Turing Institute, [Explaining decisions made with AI](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/explaining-decisions-made-with-artificial-intelligence/). Practical guidance on explaining AI-assisted decisions to affected people.

- UK Government, [UK’s Voluntary National Review of the Sustainable Development Goals](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uks-voluntary-national-review-of-the-sustainable-development-goals). The UK’s 2019 national review.
- UK Government, [UK and the Sustainable Development Goals](https://www.gov.uk/government/topical-events/uk-voluntary-national-review-of-progress-towards-the-sustainable-development-goals). Official hub for the UK Voluntary National Review process.
- Office for National Statistics, [Sustainable Development Goals: collecting and reporting UK data](https://www.ons.gov.uk/aboutus/whatwedo/programmesandprojects/sustainabledevelopmentgoals). Official information about UK SDG measurement and reporting.
- HM Treasury, [The Green Book 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book-appraisal-and-evaluation-in-central-government/the-green-book-2026). UK central-government guidance on assessing options, costs, benefits and risks.
- HM Treasury, [The Magenta Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book). UK central-government guidance on designing, conducting, using and communicating evaluation.

## Referencing standard for future contributions

When adding a factual, legal, scientific or statistical claim:

1. Prefer the original law, official statistics, primary research or authoritative institutional source.
2. Name the organisation and document.
3. Link to the exact supporting page rather than a search result or home page.
4. Record the publication or update date when it affects interpretation.
5. State whether a document is binding law, official guidance, voluntary guidance, research evidence or a proposal.
6. Record uncertainty, limitations and material disagreement.
7. Recheck time-sensitive sources before publishing a new version.
